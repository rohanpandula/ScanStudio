## Black-box acceptance cases for existing requirements

These tests are **specified, not executed**. Use ScanStudio's public UI/protocol and controlled boundary responses; no nkscan dependency, command-byte inspection, or hardware reverse engineering. Any later real-film run remains separately owner-supervised.

1. **Capability refusal:** With a production LS-5000 session advertising `[4]`, request another sample count through the public protocol. Expect `INVALID_PARAMS`, no capture started, and no completed-frame output. Request the supported recipe on an approved session and confirm effective settings in its receipt. A mock's broader settings must not enable production controls [S3].
2. **Transport/support honesty:** On an Apple Silicon configuration where FireWire discovery succeeds, confirm the scanner is identified while preview/capture remain explicitly unsupported. With the driver unavailable, the result must not claim scanning readiness. Retain app/OS, driver, scanner/holder, and adapter versions; record discovery separately from capture. This preserves current scope rather than requesting FireWire scanning [S2].
3. **Interrupted batch durability:** After one completed frame, supply a documented film-loss response during subsequent positioning. Expect the typed interruption, preservation of completed outputs/receipts, invalidation of old registration, and a fresh preview before resuming unfinished frames. Timeout reporting alone must not authorize competing motion while the original operation remains active [S1, S3].
