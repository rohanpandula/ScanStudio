# ScanStudio v0.7.0-beta.16

This release holds a metered exposure across a batch, binds multi-sampling to
what the connected scanner advertises, and replaces the ten-second control-plane
timeout with measured physical deadlines for discovering and opening the
scanner.

## Everything that changed

- Held exposure: with Auto Exposure off, the bridge meters the lowest requested
  frame in its own batch and holds that metered RGB exposure for the remaining
  frames through CoolscanPy's exposure override. Infrared is always metered.
  The inspector's auto-exposure row reads "Held from first frame" and the
  per-frame automation text explains the hold. `app/ScanStudio/protocol/BRIDGE.md`
  and `PROTOCOL.md` describe the contract.
- Multi-sampling: the picker labels a single sample "1× (off)"; the default
  stays the traced 4× so a wider advertised set never lands on a lower value
  silently. The bridge validates `multisamplePasses` against the set the opened
  device advertises and `autoExposure` as a boolean before any motion.
- Single-sample capture is lab-only. It is advertised only when ScanStudio runs
  with `SCANSTUDIO_BRIDGE_SINGLE_SAMPLE=1` and the driver declares support,
  because the live attempt was inconclusive (see Validation).
- CoolscanPy 0.7.7: `Roll.scan_many` and the batch job accept `samples_per_scan`
  (1 or 4). The traced 4-sample capture is byte-for-byte unchanged and the frame
  journal records `fine_samples_per_scan`. The vendored driver, both lockfiles,
  and the PyPI sync gate are re-pinned to the published 0.7.7 source.
- Engine deadlines: `device.open` (60 s), the startup bridge handshake (45 s),
  and `device.list` (30 s) replace the generic 10 s control-plane timeout for
  those calls. On the QA scanner `device.open` measured 8–12 s and a cold
  handshake or discovery exceeded 10 s repeatedly; each expiry quarantined the
  bridge until the app was reopened. Quarantine semantics are unchanged: a
  restart replaces only a child that has provably exited.
- `docs/reviews/next-improvements-2026-09-06.md` records every hardware run of
  the day, the USB link failures, and the open follow-ups.

## Validation

- Attended hardware runs, 2026-09-06, Nikon LS-5000 ED firmware 1.03, SA-30,
  six-frame C-41 short strip, Developer ID-signed local builds of commit
  26e53f9 (this release minus the `device.list` deadline and documentation
  commits). Evidence is kept under `~/ScanStudio-QA/single-sample-20260906/`.
- Held exposure at 4×: two frames at 4000 ppi, 16-bit RGB + infrared, ICE
  Legacy, Auto Exposure off. Frame 1 metered R 890.92 / G 1781.93 / B 1633.44 µs.
  Frame 2 was captured at exactly those values: the driver journal records
  `exposure_override.applied: true`, and its own meter would have chosen
  1466.67 / 3650.71 / 3320.72 µs. Infrared was metered per frame. Captures took
  149.2 s and 147.5 s, and all six outputs matched their receipts' SHA-256, byte
  length, and inode.
- Deadlines: with the new bounds a cold discovery took 11 s and a connect 12 s,
  and both succeeded; both had failed under the 10 s timeout earlier that day.
- Single-sample: the scanner accepted the one-sample window and metered
  normally, but the first fine READ failed with libusb OVERFLOW and the
  transport needed a power-cycle. A later plain 4× preview on the same chain of
  three cascaded USB hubs also failed a bulk read (libusb error -1). After the
  scanner was moved to a different hub chain, 4× preview and capture succeeded.
  The single-sample result is inconclusive and the mode is not offered to users.
- Local Apple Silicon suites on the release commit's tree: 530 Rust engine unit
  tests and 133 integration tests passed (the two tests requiring external
  real-image evidence remain ignored); the Swift run passed 520 tests in 55
  suites; all 399 bridge tests passed; the vendored driver suite passed 1880
  tests with 5 skipped and 1 expected failure; the 107 root policy tests, two
  packaging-policy tests, and the release-notes,
  hardware-support, scanner-contract, and release-workflow verifiers passed.
  No physical result is inferred from these tests.
- The tagged workflow independently requires the full Swift, Rust, bridge, and
  CoolscanPy suites; the CoolscanPy PyPI sync gate against the authenticated
  0.7.7 sdist; arm64 packaging; macOS 14 execution; and updater checks before
  producing and publishing the signed release. Any failed, cancelled, or skipped
  required job prevents publication.

## Platform support and installation

Download `ScanStudio-0.7.0-beta.16-macOS-arm64.dmg` for an Apple Silicon Mac
running macOS 14 (Sonoma) or newer. The release workflow Developer ID-signs,
notarizes, and staples both the app and DMG. Copy ScanStudio to Applications.
The app contains the hardware bridge, bundled runtime and libusb, corresponding
GPL source, and license notices.

Intel Macs, Windows, and Linux are retired ScanStudio platforms. Consult
[NegPy downloads](https://github.com/marcinz606/NegPy/releases) and
[upstream compatibility guidance](https://github.com/marcinz606/NegPy#readme).
A downloadable package does not guarantee a particular scanner configuration.

## Known limitations

The final physical full-roll acceptance run is not part of this release. The
2026-09-06 runs are short-strip runs on one Mac and one scanner, not a
full-roll pass.
Held exposure was validated on two frames of one strip. The anchor is the
lowest requested frame, so a batch that starts on a thin frame holds a short
exposure for denser frames; the held frame 2 was visibly darker than its
earlier per-frame capture. Choose the anchor frame deliberately.
Single-sample (1×) capture remains lab-only until a verified single-sample USB
trace exists on a reliable link.
Cascaded USB hubs produced intermittent bulk-read failures (libusb -1, -8,
OVERFLOW, and one ENODEV) that the app reports but does not diagnose; the
scanner is USB-A only and could not be connected directly.
Repeated eject and reinsert of a short strip produced one REFEED_REQUIRED
preview and one pre-motion batch refusal worded "live mapping has fewer than 2
scanner-addressable frame records"; that wording is a follow-up.
Positive renders still use the default blind exposure estimator; no reference
scan exists to grade them.
The LS-5000 color-roll workflow remains Beta with limited retained hardware
coverage. Additional firmware, holders, adapters, and macOS configurations need
supervised evidence. LS-40/LS-50 and FireWire models remain unsupported for
scanning; physical validation issues are not represented as completed.

Simulator images cannot prove real focus, framing, color, infrared repair, or
film transport. Continuous monitoring does not authorize automatic physical
rescans, refeeding, or competing commands while a scan is active. Use the
[Mac acceptance guide](../MAC-ACCEPTANCE.md) for the final full-roll run and
recovery procedure.
